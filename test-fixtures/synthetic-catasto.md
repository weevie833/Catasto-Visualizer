# Casale Esempio 1760 — synthetic test transcription

Invented for tests. Every name, place, and figure below is made up; it is not taken from any
real Catasto source. It exercises the parser and exports: both languages, an entry that crosses
a page, a widow's children, an infant, an Italian patronymic and origin, residence and land
borders, and a creditor of another town.
