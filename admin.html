Best Practices for Creating a Project

The Catasto Visualizer is designed to import a plain text transcript in either Italian or English, but the format of your document needs to follow some strict rules. The formatting rules allow the parser to accurately interpret your transcript into the different classifications of content.

Catasto content only

Keep the transcript free of anything but the actual content of the Catasto Onciario register. If you have added any preamble or introduction, delete it.

Header formatting

The transcript needs to include headers that distinguish the start of each source image’s content, such as:

--- START OF RECORD: Lappano Catasto Onciario 1742_010.png ---

The key terms are “START OF RECORD” and the number at the end. This allows the app to tell you where an entry is located in the visualizer.

Entry formatting

The start of a household begins with the list of the head of household. The list of household members should look like this:

Antonio Bafaro, day laborer, age 28.
Giuseppe, brother, day laborer, age 24.
Pascale, brother, age 9.
Livia, sister, unmarried woman, age 16.
Rosa, sister, unmarried woman, age 14.
Anna, sister, unmarried woman, age 12.
Catarina Scarpella, widow, age 50.
Pompea, sister, married, age 26.
Nicolò Imbrogno, day laborer, husband of Pompea, brother-in-law — 32.

Head of Antonio — 1.
Industry of the same — ounces 12.
Industry of Giuseppe — ounces 12.
Industry of Nicolò — ounces 12.

Lives in own House. Owns, as per declaration page 6th

A Chestnut grove called la Fontana di in capo of annual income 1. as in appraisal page 13. number 216. — 3. 10.

The rest of the entry can be plain text, as-is. If you upload a DOCX, MD, or TXT file and the Admin says that no households can be found, it is because of the formatting.

Variations in Catasto content

Not every Catasto Onciario contains relationship information, such as adjacent households or landholdings. If the transcript does not indicate any, then the visualizer cannot show relationships. However, you can still refer to the Adjacent Evidence to locate potential connections.

Replacing a project

If you discover that your transcript contains errors after you import it into the app, make your corrections in your source transcript, delete the old project out of the Admin, and re-upload your transcript as a new project. Keep your source transcript as the “single version of the truth.”
